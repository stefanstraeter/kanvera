Welcome to Join

Join is your intuitive Kanban-based project management tool, designed to visualize work, limit work-in-progress, and maximize your team's efficiency. Inspired by the Japanese "billboard" principle, we help you keep track of every task in a clean, visual interface.

Note: This application was built as an educational project and is not intended for extensive business usage.

How to use Join

Getting started is easy. Just follow these four simple steps:

  • Explore the Board: Your project is divided into four stages: To Do, In Progress, Await Feedback, and Done.
  • Manage Contacts: Head over to the Contacts section to build your team. Add names and info to assign them to tasks later.
  • Create Tasks: Click the "+" button on the board to add a new task card. Fill in the title, description, and assign your team members.
  • Flow with Drag & Drop: Simply drag a card to the next column as your work progresses.

Managing Cards

Every card is interactive. You can open a card to see all details, edit the information, or delete it once it's no longer needed. But be careful: Deleting a card is permanent and cannot be undone.
